Database(s) Status

Enter your Super Admin credentials above, then click Refresh to view database status.

Visit and Issue numbers are tracked by this app separately from the spreadsheet itself - that's deliberate, it's what lets numbering carry on smoothly across an "Add New Database" switch without restarting. Add New Database Now creates a brand-new spreadsheet, copies your clinic settings, physiotherapist accounts, issues list and patients into it, archives the current one as read-only patient-visit history, and switches every new record there - immediately, with no Apps Script editor involved. Reset Numbering & Forget Old Archives is only for fixing numbering after manually editing the sheet, or undoing a test switch - it does not delete any spreadsheet or any row.
