Database(s) Status

Enter your Super Admin credentials above, then click Refresh to view database status.

Invoice, Patient and Product numbers are tracked by this app separately from the spreadsheet itself - that's deliberate, it's what lets numbering carry on smoothly across an "Add New Database" switch. If you ever edit a spreadsheet directly (clear rows by hand, test "Make a copy"), those numbers won't automatically notice - click "Refresh Numbering" to re-sync them from what's actually in the sheet(s) without losing any linked archives, or "Reset Numbering & Forget Old Archives" if you no longer need those archives at all. Once a spreadsheet is archived, its invoices become read-only (Find/Edit Bill will show them but won't allow changes) - the same principle as not editing a closed financial year's books.

Change the colors used everywhere in this app - the login screen, menubar/sidebar, buttons, page headings, tabs, dashboard/inventory charts and number cards, the Find/Edit highlight box, and the bill header and typography (company name + invoice number on every bill, both on screen and when shared by print, PDF or email). Every option below shows a live preview right next to it. Changes apply for every biller and device the moment you click Save - no redeploy, no code, needed. This only ever changes colors, fonts and layout - nothing here can affect how the app works.
